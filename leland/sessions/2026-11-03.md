## Correction: which free-space number to use
- I first quoted 36.5 GB (plain free space). Finder says 69.42 GB. **Finder's number is the right one.**
- It is the figure macOS gives apps that install things, measured at 69.41 GB, the same as Finder.
- The ~33 GB difference is space macOS empties on its own when an app needs room: app caches
  (~24 GB in `~/Library/Caches`) and local copies of iCloud/OneDrive/Dropbox files (~9 GB), which go
  back to "download on demand". Not virtual memory: the 6 GB swap file is already counted as used.
- From now on, disk figures are Finder's.
