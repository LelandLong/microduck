  app; building a replica.
- **Option 4, training my own tricks, explained.** A *policy* is a small neural network: 61 numbers
  in (joint angles, tilt, commanded speed…), 14 motor targets out, 50 times a second. *Reinforcement
  learning* trains it by trial and error against a *reward* (a score I design). *PPO* is the
  algorithm. Thousands of simulated ducks run in parallel on a GPU. The result is exported as ONNX
  and loaded onto the duck. Two routes:
  - Official `microduck_rl`: needs an NVIDIA GPU, ~1–2 h for a usable walk.
  - Community `jonathanhawkins/microduck-lab`: trains on a Mac CPU in minutes; a prototype for
    trying reward ideas, not for the real robot.
- **Hugging Face Jobs pricing** (cloud GPU): L4 $0.80/h (microduck_rl's default), A10G-large $1.50/h,
  A100 $2.50/h; billed per minute; prepaid credit; default 12 h timeout caps a run at $9.60. PRO not
  needed.
