How it will work:

  • Switched on from the laptop (Wake-on-LAN, over the wired home network). Needs two settings checked on the PC: one in the BIOS, one on the network adapter.
  • Every training run ends by shutting the PC down. No idling, so less heat and fan noise in the office.
  • Optional: cap the graphics card's power (about 170 W → 120 W): much quieter and cooler, perhaps 10–20% slower.
  • Then ssh pc from the laptop, just like the Studio.

Linux on the PC (WSL2): Linux runs inside Windows as one big file, with no partitioning and no choosing Windows or Linux at startup. It goes on the second drive (wsl --install --location D:\WSL\…), grows as needed, and needs about 20–50 GB. Pollen's training doesn't work in plain Windows (Kevin Wood's video shows it failing) but does in WSL2.
